# Portal surface brief

Mode: Operate

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Make the 14-day working trial legible as one shared operation, with the MAVI operator able to catch access friction before it delays first value. Avoid separate, disconnected dashboards for the three participants.

**OWN-WORLD:** MAVI's bright violet mark and calls to action sit on white and ink-black surfaces. Use a compact finance-operations grid, crisp typography, hairline rules, and tabular time/status values. Violet marks identity and current action; red and amber remain risk states. No multicolor gradients. The supplied MAVI homepage's dotted orbit and layered candidate/job imagery inform only a restrained, functional status connection—not a decorative clone.

**STORY:** The operator opens on a 14-day timeline and sees Ramp access overdue by 52 hours. The operator records a simulated nudge, then switches to customer and candidate views to understand what each person sees and does. The three views share one local trial state.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** At desktop width, a compact MAVI header holds the wordmark, illustrative-data notice, Athena Club scenario selector, three-role switcher, day control, and reset action. Below it, a clear trial title and violet/neutral status strip lead into one 14-day ruler with three aligned horizontal lanes: customer, candidate, operator. Phase columns align across all lanes. A single rose overdue-access alert spans the operator lane and visually connects to the pending access task above. The first screen shows the issue, owner, elapsed time, and next action without scrolling.

**FORM:** The selected three-lane trial map (surface seed `a2d51e51`, grounded direction 4 of 7). The role switcher changes the main participant workspace; the operator view opens first. Keep the timeline as the shared spine, not three independent pages. Cross-role reports feed an operator intervention queue. On mobile, retain the role selector and collapse each lane into a chronological stack without losing phase labels or alert ownership.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## QUALITY BAR

- Desktop and mobile layouts keep all three roles and the 48-hour access alert easy to find.
- The violet brand accent is recognizable but does not replace neutral reading surfaces or health colors.
- Every displayed claim, score, profile, security state, and notification is either supplied and verified or explicitly illustrative.
- Controls work by keyboard, have visible focus, and do not use color alone to convey status.
- The interface has no dead-end buttons, fake external sends, or auth implications.
